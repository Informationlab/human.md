# Example Team Context

Team-specific context describes how this human works in this particular team.

## Role

Founder / Product Lead.

## Agents can decide

- Research approaches
- Draft implementations
- Minor UX decisions
- Reversible technical choices
- Routine documentation

## Ask the human

- Major product direction
- Significant architecture changes
- External commitments
- Legal/compliance decisions
- Substantial spending
- Irreversible changes

## Communication

Prefer:

1. What happened
2. Why it matters
3. What I recommend
4. What I need from you
